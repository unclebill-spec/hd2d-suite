# Hearthmoor Story Seeds (saved 2026-10-01)

Bill likes all three. Undecided; keep all. Favorite mechanic: **opening and closing portals changes how the game plays out** (Rootfall core, usable in any mix).

## 1. The Dimming
Hearth-spirits that warm the valley's lamps and springs are falling asleep. Cause: magic slowly fading. The Lantern Thief, a gloomy figure, is speeding it up for a hidden reason. Gods: small household and nature spirits. Player: the new lamplighter, waking spirits so the world brightens. Companions: a grumpy gnome, a will-o'-wisp, a fox spirit. Myth: made-up cozy, Shire-like.

## 2. The Rootfall
A great mushroom world-tree under the land cracked, and spore portals opened. Good effects: new springs, glowing fungus forests, friendly gnome tribes. Bad effects: spore-blighted animals, towns cut off. No single villain; mystery: who cracked the root. The player chooses which portals to seal or keep open, and those choices change the map. Creatures: mushroom folk, moss golems, spring kelpies, moth spirits. Myth: original Celtic/Norse-flavored fey folklore (no real gods).

## 3. Lantern of the Eight Winds
A celestial lantern that guided the seasons shattered; its 8 wind-spirits scattered, unbalancing regions. Villain: a rival lamp-keeper after the shards. Each spirit returned becomes a companion and a power. Creatures: kitsune-like foxes, cloud carp, tanuki merchants, paper lantern spirits. Myth: East Asian-inspired but invented.

## Bill's theme loves
Mushrooms, gnomes, hidden bubbly springs, night cycles, neon-pulsing wisps and fireflies, torches casting shadows, orb lanterns with glowing fish. Portals drawn fresh from the rift_refs style.

# CHOSEN DIRECTION (Bill, 2026-10-01): Norse Nine Realms
- Myth: Norse-inspired (public-domain folklore, all art and characters original). Nine realms linked by the **Rainbow Rift** (a Bifröst-like bridge of portals).
- Inciting threat: a villain from one realm is trying to start a civil war among the realms.
- Player crosses the Rainbow Rift realm by realm. Per realm: **conquer** (kill your way through, which makes the realm a permanent enemy and closes off its companions, crafted treasures, and spells) OR **befriend** (quests that still involve fighting and problem-solving: clear an evil spirit, restore a polluted spring, return a missing egg, recover a stolen artifact, cleanse a temple).
- Realm rivalries: some realms hate each other, so you can't befriend them all. Allying with one can lock out its rival.
- Companions recruited from befriended realms.
- Magic: elemental, light, and dark, plus a unique magic per realm.
- Entrances: each realm has its own entrance. Some must be fought through; some open freely depending on current alliances. Opening and closing portals changes how the game plays out.
- Midgard = Hearthmoor (home hub).
- Realms: Asgard (light/thunder/runes), Vanaheim (nature/growth/foresight), Alfheim (light elves, wisps), Midgard (home), Jotunheim (frost/stone giants), Svartalfheim/Nidavellir (dwarves, forge, crafting), Muspelheim (fire), Niflheim (ice/mist/water), Helheim (dead, dark magic).

## Villain (draft, 2026-10-01): Veyra Ashmantle, the Rift-Warden (Niflheim)
- Last of Niflheim's frost-witches, who guarded and stabilized the Rainbow Rift.
- Backstory: the rift cracked in a fire/ice skirmish; Muspelheim blamed Niflheim, Asgard sided with fire, Niflheim was cut off to freeze; her sister died in the collapse.
- Goal: prove the realms' unity is fake by making them destroy each other, then seize the Rift and remake the nine realms into one frozen, ordered kingdom.
- Methods (= befriend quest hooks): poisons Vanaheim springs with Alfheim runes planted; steals a dwarven artifact and plants it on the giants; corrupts temples to frame neighbors.
- Each befriend quest undoes a frame-up and becomes evidence against her; conquering realms advances her plan (she taunts the player).
- Befriending Niflheim may unlock a late redeem-vs-defeat choice.
- Combat: frost/mist illusion clones, freezing portals shut, turning realm guardians; final battle on the freezing Rainbow Rift.

## Realm alliance map (draft, 2026-10-01; Bill approved Veyra)
Rival pairs (befriend one, the other turns hostile):
- Alfheim (light magic, wisp companion) vs Svartalfheim (forge, crafted treasures, dwarf smith companion)
- Muspelheim (fire, salamander companion) vs Niflheim (frost/mist, frost-witch companion, Veyra redemption path)
- Asgard (thunder/runes, valkyrie-style companion) vs Jotunheim (stone/frost strength, gentle giant companion)
Swing realm: Vanaheim (growth/healing, gnome or moss-folk companion); Asgard ally makes it harder (old Aesir-Vanir war), Jotunheim ally makes it easier.
Neutral: Helheim (never ally or enemy; dangerous pass-through; dark magic at a cost; fallen allies go there and one can be won back). Midgard = Hearthmoor home hub, look changes with alliances.
Entrances: allied realms open freely; neutral realms need a key quest; hostile realms must be fought through (guarded gate or cracked side portal).
Max 4 allies out of 7 (one per rival pair + Vanaheim); at least 3 enemies, which Veyra rallies for the finale.

## Hero (draft, 2026-10-01)
Pick 1 of 6 origins (each has starting magic plus a realm bias):
- Mortal: Midgard farmhand (no magic, learns any magic faster, neutral everywhere); Shield-warden (Hearthmoor guard, best fighter, Jotunheim respects you); Rune-reader (village seer, rune magic, reads Veyra's planted clues more easily).
- Demigod: Child of thunder (Asgard storm god parent, lightning; Asgard friendly, Jotunheim hostile); Child of the Vanir (nature goddess parent, healing/growth; Vanaheim and mushroom folk friendly); Ember-born (fire-giant parent, fire; Muspelheim friendly, Niflheim hostile, Veyra resents you).
Main branch (Veyra recruits you mid-game):
- Rift-Saver: undo her frame-ups, unite realms, defeat or redeem her on the frozen Rift.

Endings combine origin, side, and alliances: united realms / frozen kingdom / Veyra redeemed / you rule the Rift.

## Merit pacing (2026-10-02). Merit must feel earned
- Ranks per faction: Stranger, Friend, Trusted, Honored, Champion, Legend. Each rank roughly 3-5 meaningful activities; Legend rank = faction legendary item/quest.
- Activity sources per city (every city offers several faction tasks): faction quest chains (big merit), bounty board (elite hunts, rotating), rift closures / dungeon clears, rescue & escort, gathering/crafting orders, hidden-spring & secret finds, arena or trial challenges, nightly events (night cycle).
- Gear costs scale with rank; best gear needs both rank and currency (no grinding one thing, mix activities). Small daily/weekly bonuses, no paywalls.
- Rival-faction tension: Corsair merit lowers Order of the Hearth standing, etc.

## Random rift events (2026-10-02)
- Rifts open randomly across all realms (warning: sky crack, cold-fire glow, rumble). Entering leads to a short pocket dimension of "old evils" aligned with no faction (ancient horrors, forgotten gods' remnants, rogue golems, lost wraith legions).
- Clearing a rift grants merit to the faction of the realm you're currently in (plus Gatekeepers' Guild Rift Marks).
- Tiers: minor (quick), major (mini-boss), and rare Abyssal Rift (boss + legendary-material drop). Rifts close if ignored; harder at night. Portal art from rift_refs, drawn fresh.

## Random rares & mini-bosses (2026-10-02)
- Rare named monsters and mini-bosses spawn randomly in the overworld of every realm and inside rifts (e.g. a glowing golem variant, a lone lich, an elder wraith).
- Tell: name plate + distinct neon glow (blue/violet/red) and a sound cue; some only appear at night or in weather.
- Rewards: faction merit (realm you're in), decent gear, crafting materials, small chance of a rare cosmetic or pet. Tracked in a bestiary "hunt log".

## Dungeons, procedural rares & rift types (2026-10-02)
- Static dungeons: hand-built story dungeons (1-2 per realm city region) with fixed bosses and set loot.
- Procedural dungeons: seeded room-and-corridor layouts using realm tile kits; random rooms, traps, secrets, hidden springs; replayable for merit.
- Procedural rares and under-bosses: generated from base monster + element affix (blue cold fire / violet / red neon) + trait (e.g. Shielded, Splitting, Summoner, Blinking, Enraged); scattered across all worlds.
- Rift bosses: drawn from a shared pool (random) plus a few fixed signature bosses per rift type.
- Five starting rift types:
  1. Frostfire Rift: blue cold-fire wraiths, ice golems, frost liches.
  2. Gloam Rift: shades, skeleton lines, death knights (violet).
  3. Bloodmoon Rift: vampires, blood golems, red-neon demon lords.
  4. Storm Rift: lightning golems, storm elementals, fallen angels and nephilim.
  5. Ruin Rift: ancient stone/flesh golems, forgotten-god relics, mixed old evils.
- Loot: one shared base table (gold, materials, common gear) + semi-shared per rift type/realm (themed gear, element mats) + small unique drops per fixed boss.

## Gear rarity (2026-10-02). 5 tiers, traditional colors
- Common (white/gray), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Legendary (orange/gold).
- Applies to armor, weapons, and trinkets. Higher tiers add more bonus stats and affixes; Epic+ get a faint glow on the sprite, Legendary gets an animated neon aura and a unique effect.
- Loot beams/name colors match tier; drop rates scale with rift tier, rares, and bosses.

## Upgrades, rerolls & sockets (2026-10-02)
- Upgrade: armor and weapons upgrade +1 to +10 at forges using materials (found or bought); cap set by rarity so a Common can't out-scale a Legendary.
- Reroll: rune stones (found in the world, rifts, or bought from factions) reroll one bonus stat at a time; can lock a stat for extra cost. Limited rerolls per item.
- Sockets: 1-3 sockets by rarity. Socket gems/runes/cores into armor and weapons for special shields (frost barrier, ember ward, light aegis), on-hit effects (chain lightning, life drain, cold-fire burn), and auras. Golem cores and rift shards are high-tier socketables.
- Sockets can be cleared at a jeweler (Gnome Council does it cheaper).

## Banking, merchants, travel, mounts, housing (2026-10-02)
- Bank: shared vault across all cities (expandable tabs), gold storage, materials bag; Gnome Council safe-deposit for rare items.
- Merchants: city shops with realm stock, rotating rare traders, a sell-back/buyback tab, faction vendors, a traveling night merchant, and the Wayfarer's Market at Bifrost.
- Fast travel: rune waystones per city/district, unlocked by visiting; Bifrost gates between realms (allied realms free, others need keys); Gatekeepers' Guild discounts.
- Mounts: realm-themed (storm stag, moss boar, frost elk, ember lizard, griffin late-game); some from faction ranks, rares, or quests; mounts glow at night.
- Housing: rent an inn room in every realm city (rest, storage, save); buy real homes in a few places (Hearthmoor cottage, Ravenhold townhouse, Bifrost tower suite), upgradeable and decoratable, with a pet corner and trophy wall.
