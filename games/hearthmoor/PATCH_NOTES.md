# Hearthmoor patch notes

Player-facing notes, newest first.

## Pause menu, minimap and the new gear (November 2026)

- **Pause menu:** press Esc (or Start on a gamepad) to pause. It used to open the quest log; the Quest log is now a button inside the pause menu, next to Character, Options and save / load.
- **Minimap:** a small map in the top-right corner shows where you are. Tap it to pause.
- **Cursor and font:** a new pixel cursor, and an easier-to-read menu font (Atkinson Hyperlegible).
- **Classic menu colours:** menus are back to the warm parchment and wood look.
- **HUD buttons:** the save, options and pause chips are gone from the screen edge; there is a small pause button by your bag and a big seed-bomb button in the lower right.
- **14 gear slots:** helm, neck, chest, legs, feet, arms, gloves, two rings, belt, cloak, main hand, off-hand and ranged / charm. Compare any piece with what you wear before you equip it.
- **5 rarities:** Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and the new **Runeforged**, which drops with a pink rune beam (mostly from bosses).
- **Your old gear comes with you:** weapons move to the main hand, armor to the chest, trinkets to the neck. Uncommon pieces become Common, and stats are adjusted to match new gear of the same rarity and level. Gems, sockets, tempering and names are kept; unique items are unchanged.
